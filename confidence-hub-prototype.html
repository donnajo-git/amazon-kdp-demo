How to use this view

Reading progress is most useful when viewed over time or across the author’s own titles. A decline at any point is not automatically negative. Readers may pause, return later, skip non-story material, or stop for reasons this report cannot observe.

Reading-progress data is based on aggregated, privacy-protected Kindle reading activity that meets reporting and data-quality requirements. It does not identify individual readers, reveal specific reading behavior, explain why activity changes, or measure the quality of a chapter or book.
